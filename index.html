Good to know.

Quick answers to the usual questions.

Do you need access to water and power?

Yes, we need access to both. We’ll set everything up ourselves, so all we ask is a spot with room to work around the car.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. As long as the car is accessible and we’ve agreed the details, you can carry on with your day.

How long does it take?

An exterior or interior detail is typically a couple of hours. The full detail takes longer, and heavily soiled vehicles may need extra time.

What if it rains?

Interior details can go ahead regardless. For exterior work we’ll happily move your booking to a dry day at no charge.

How do payments and cancellations work?

Payment is taken in full when you book. Cancel more than 3 days before your detail and we’ll either move you to the next available date or refund you in full, at no charge. Cancellations made within 3 days of the detail are non-refundable.
